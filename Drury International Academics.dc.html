Your Drury Fusion

Fusion is the curriculum that blends professional credentials with personal interests, so you graduate with proof of what you can do — not just a transcript. Three things are true for every student:

01

Majors, minors, and certificates

More than 80 to combine. Pair computer science with a Spanish minor, or accounting with a cyber-risk certificate.

02

Three real-world projects

Every student completes at least three: service learning, an internship, undergraduate research, or study abroad. This is graded coursework, not an extracurricular you have to find on your own.

03

A mentor at the Compass Center

Career coaching, internship placement, and graduate school planning in one office — available from your first semester, not your last.

Architecture

Most students earn the M.Arch. in five years rather than the national average of six. Every student studies abroad for at least five weeks and completes 360 paid internship hours. Alumni work in 41 states and 16 countries.

Pre-Med & Pre-Health

Drury's professional school acceptance rates run well above national averages. The curriculum was built by studying the admission requirements of the top schools in the Midwest: a shared core, then eleven tracks from pre-medicine to physician assistant, with electives that let you pursue other interests. Early acceptance to Missouri medical and dental schools is available through partner scholars programs, which reduce or remove the MCAT as a selection factor.

Biochemistry

A 63-credit BS spanning chemistry, biology, calculus, and physics, with labs at every level and a senior capstone. Advanced options include medicinal chemistry, instrumental analysis, immunology, and cellular and molecular biology. Biochemistry majors cannot also major or minor in biology or chemistry.

Psychology

Nearly 100% of psychology students who apply are accepted to graduate, law, or medical school, recently at Washington University, UC Davis, Purdue, and King's College London. Two degrees: the BS research track (47–50 credits, with a two-semester thesis) for graduate study, and the BA applied track (44 credits, two internships) for business, education, law, or social work. Every student completes at least one professional internship.

Engineering

A BS in electrical or mechanical engineering, completed in four years at a maximum of 17 credit hours per semester. A common first year — embedded systems, computer-aided design and 3D printing, interdisciplinary design, MATLAB — lets you choose your major after seeing the breadth of engineering. All labs and lectures are taught by full-time faculty, both of whom are ABET accreditation evaluators with decades of teaching behind them. Note the engineering program fee: $600 per class, capped at $1,200 per semester.

Exercise Physiology

A 66-credit BS built for doctoral programs in physical therapy and graduate study in exercise physiology. Coursework spans biology, chemistry, physics, and mathematics, including human anatomy, human physiology, nutrition, strength training, and prevention and care of athletic injuries. A required internship and senior seminar close out the degree.

Music Therapy

An 87-credit Bachelor of Music Therapy for students who want to work clinically, using music to reach non-musical goals in cognition, communication, movement, and emotional health. Coursework combines music theory and performance with psychology, four rounds of field studies, and an internship. Practical training happens at Drury's own Center for Music Therapy and Wellness, and graduates are eligible to sit the board exam for the MT-BC credential. All music majors must pass a faculty audition.

Chemistry

A hands-on program built around modern equipment and instrumentation, offered as three separate majors: ACS Chemistry, Biochemistry, and Chemistry. The 65-credit ACS Chemistry BS is approved by the American Chemical Society and aimed at research careers or professional practice; the 44-credit Chemistry BA leaves more room for another field. Both prepare you for graduate school or a health professional programme. Talk to a chemistry advisor early about which fits your career.

Academic quality

Drury is a private university in Springfield, Missouri, built around small classes and direct contact with faculty. Your academic record is the strongest predictor of your success here, and your interests outside the classroom say a lot about how you'll contribute to the community.

ACCREDITATION

Drury University is accredited by the Higher Learning Commission. The Breech School of Business is accredited by AACSB, an honor only 5% of the world’s business programs hold. Other programs, including architecture and nursing, hold professional accreditation through their own national bodies.

Accreditation matters for credential recognition when you return home or apply to graduate school. Ask your counselor for a letter naming the specific accrediting body for your program if your ministry of education requires one.

Undergraduate research and honors

Undergraduate research is one of the real-world experiences that counts toward your Fusion requirement, which means it's built into the degree rather than something you have to find on your own.

WORK WITH FACULTY

Research opportunities exist across the sciences, social sciences, and humanities, and honors students are supported in presenting their work at academic conferences. Ask a department directly about openings — your counselor can make the introduction before you even apply.

HONORS

The program looks for students who are curious, independent, ambitious, hard-working, and intellectually engaged with the world of ideas. Its guiding line comes from Socrates: wisdom begins in wonder. Over 30+ years it has built a record of students who take engaging honors courses, conduct original research and present it at academic conferences, work closely with accomplished faculty, and compete for prestigious fellowships and scholarships for graduate school. Graduates leave with research and communication skills that give them an edge in their profession or graduate program, and a Degree with Honors.

COUNTS AS FUSION

A research project satisfies one of your three required real-world experiences, and gives you a faculty reference who has actually seen you work.

Study abroad

Study abroad is one of the most exciting opportunities available to Drury students and is described by many as the experience of a lifetime. All Drury students have the option to study abroad — including international students.

Who goes

Required for Breech School of Business and Hammons School of Architecture students. Optional for everyone else. Most students go during their junior year, though sophomore year is possible.

Program lengths

Full semester, or short-term programs in winter and summer terms. Recent destinations include Sri Lanka and Italy.

Cost and aid

Your financial aid and scholarships can apply toward certain semester programs. The Office for International Programs awards additional study abroad scholarships for both semester and short-term programs. These are limited — apply as early as you can.

Visa note

On an F-1 visa, leaving and re-entering the US needs planning: a valid travel signature on your I-20, and in some cases a visa renewal appointment. Talk to the study abroad programs director before you commit to a program.
