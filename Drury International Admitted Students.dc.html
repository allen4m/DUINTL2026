I-20 & visa

Once we receive your enrollment deposit and housing application, we begin processing your I-20 and guiding you through the F-1 visa interview. This step is time sensitive and often takes longer than students expect, so pay your deposit and submit your housing application as soon as you are admitted. The visa process now also includes a social media vetting step conducted by the U.S. embassy or consulate.

  • ›
    Receive your I-20 from Drury
    Issued once your documents are complete. The day it arrives, check your name and date of birth against your passport, then review the program/major, and the financial figures. Ask your counselor about anything that looks wrong or unclear before moving forward with paying the SEVIS fee.
  • ›
    Pay the I-901 SEVIS fee
    Paid to the U.S. government after you receive the I-20. Keep the payment confirmation (receipt).
  • ›
    Schedule your visa interview
    Complete Form DS-160, then book your interview at the U.S. embassy or consulate in your region. Waits vary widely from one post to another, so take the earliest date offered even if it is inconvenient, and tell us when it is. Check current visa wait times(opens in a new tab).
CHECKLIST FOR VISA INTERVIEW
  • ›I-20, DS-160 & I-901 payment confirmation
  • ›Passport and acceptance letter
  • ›Bank document
  • ›Official transcripts
IF YOUR VISA IS DELAYED

Contact International Admission immediately. We can often defer your start to the next term and reissue your I-20 rather than have you lose the admission and scholarship you have already earned.

Set up OneLogin

OneLogin is your single sign-on for Drury applications, and it requires multi-factor authentication. Set it up before you leave home: changing phone numbers or SIM cards after arrival makes it harder.

DESKTOP REQUIRED

First-time setup must be completed on a desktop computer.

STEP 1 · SIGN IN

Begin at drury.onelogin.com(opens in a new tab) and enter your Drury email or username — the username is the part of the email before @drury.edu. Review the agreement and click Accept.

STEP 2 · CHOOSE YOUR AUTHENTICATION FACTOR

Setting an authentication factor is required. You may select more than one and change these options later.

  • ›
    OneLogin Protect app — very secure, recommended
  • ›
    Microsoft Authenticator — very secure
  • ›
    Google Authenticator — very secure
  • ›
    Alternate email — less secure. Use a non-Drury address; it receives your login verification token and is also used for password resets.
STEP 3 · SET UP ONELOGIN PROTECT
  1. Download the OneLogin Protect app from your phone's app store.
  2. Open the app and select the + to add a new account.
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone.
  4. Once the app is installed, click Activate on the desktop screen, then select Accept in the OneLogin Protect app.
STEP 4 · SET YOUR PASSWORD

Set your OneLogin/Drury password. It must be at least 14 characters and include one capital letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character.

After your initial login, OneLogin may ask you to download the OneLogin browser extension, which controls password management. This is optional. You then have access to all applications.

Paying for Drury

Accept your awards in MyDrury once they appear, and decide how you will pay the balance. Tuition must be paid in full, or covered by a payment plan, by August 1.

ACCEPT YOUR AWARDS

Sign in to MyDrury(opens in a new tab) and open the Financial Aid tab in the grey menu bar, then My Awards Info and the ACCESS Financial Aid Data tab.

  1. Read and tick the Terms and Conditions box, then Submit.
  2. Check that the award year is correct and open Menu at the top left to see your awards.
  3. Accept or decline each award individually, or use Accept All or Decline All, then Submit.
Full instructions with screenshots →(opens in a new tab)
PAYMENT PLAN

If you are paying over the term rather than in one payment, enrol in a Nelnet payment plan through MyDrury: Student tab, then Nelnet Payment Plan, choose the year and semester, and click Pay using Nelnet. You can also enrol online directly(opens in a new tab).

  • ›
    Monthly payments with no interest, by automatic bank payment or card.
  • ›
    Payments are processed on the 5th of each month until the balance is paid in full.
  • ›
    $50 enrolment fee per semester. You re-enrol each semester.

Card payments carry a service fee: 2.95% domestic, 4.25% international, with a $3.00 minimum on transactions between $20 and $106. Electronic check is $0.50 per transaction.

FOR INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS

U.S. federal aid, including the FAFSA and federal loans, is generally not available to international students. Your Drury scholarship and any external funding are what you accept in MyDrury.

5

Your class schedule

In the spring you will receive an email with instructions to complete a pre-scheduling questionnaire, which is used to build your first class schedule. It asks about your academic background and interests.

Registration order is prioritized by the date your questionnaire is received, so submit it as soon as it arrives. Your admission counselor will make sure you do not miss it.

Required forms

All required forms are completed through MyDrury: sign in with your Drury username and password, click the Student tab, then Student Forms in the left sidebar. If you start a form and run out of time, you can save it and return later.

REGISTRATION HOLD

Students who do not submit these requirements may have a registration hold placed on their account.

  • ›
    Information release (FERPA)
    If you are 18 or older, this form authorises Drury to release your personal, academic and financial information to people you name. Without it, federal law prevents faculty and staff from releasing any information about you to anyone, including parents.
  • ›
    Health and emergency information form
    If you are involved in a campus-related emergency, this information is passed to the health professionals caring for you. It stays private and on file otherwise.
  • ›
    New student housing contract, or housing exemption request
    All full-time day school students live on campus unless they meet an exemption criterion: age 21 or older at the start of the academic year, married, have a child, a medical condition that cannot reasonably be accommodated in student housing, extenuating circumstances, or veteran or current U.S. military service. Students 21 and over receive priority for off-campus housing but are not guaranteed approval.
  • ›
    Vaccination records
    Submit a copy of your complete records to the Executive Assistant to the Vice President of Student Affairs / Dean of Students. Required: MMR (measles, mumps, rubella), polio, and DPT (diphtheria, pertussis, tetanus). Recommended: hepatitis B, varicella (chicken pox), and meningococcal (meningitis). Bring your original records with you when you travel.

The engineering program requires a Windows PC — some required software will not run on a Mac. Recommended: Intel i7 or AMD Ryzen 7 or higher, 16GB+ RAM, a 1TB SSD, an Nvidia RTX 3000 or higher, a 16-inch or larger display, and at least two USB ports. A machine meeting these specs should last all four years, so buy before you travel if you can.
