Deposit & housing

Your enrollment deposit holds your place in the class and starts registration and housing. Nothing else on this list moves until it is paid.

$500
INTERNATIONAL STUDENT DEPOSIT

The enrollment deposit for international students. It is non-refundable, but it is not an extra fee — it is credited to your student account once you enrol.

What comes next

Once your deposit and housing application are in, everything else follows in order: your I-20 and visa interview, your Drury account, accepting awards and arranging payment, registration, required forms, and travel. That whole sequence lives on one page.
