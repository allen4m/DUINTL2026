Come Home to Campus

There is something particular about Drury’s campus — trees moving in the breeze, conversations on Drury Lane, students gathering around Sunderland Field. What makes it different is not the beauty of the ninety acres but the people. You will know your professors and recognize faces wherever you go.

Springfield offers a diverse food scene, concerts, art festivals, and a walkable downtown, with nearly a hundred public parks and six lakes a short drive away. Springfield-Branson National Airport flies nonstop to fifteen destinations, including Los Angeles, Denver, Atlanta, and Chicago.
