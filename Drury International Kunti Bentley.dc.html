Support for every part of student life

The Office of Student Support Services provides personalized guidance, resources, and advocacy to help all students thrive academically, socially, and personally.

Kunti works with students one to one and connects them to the right people on campus and in Springfield. If you are not sure who to ask about something, start here.

Services

One-on-one mentoring & advocacy A consistent person who knows your situation and can speak on your behalf.
Student life navigation Help working out which office handles what, and how to get something done.
Campus & community referrals Connections to services at Drury and to resources in Springfield.
Affinity group mentorship Mentorship within student communities who share a background or experience.
Faculty, staff & community collaboration Working with instructors and partners to put support in place around you.
Community engagement & resources Ways to take part in life beyond campus, and the resources that make it possible.
