Before you start

HOW TO SHORTEN YOUR WAIT

How you submit your documents shapes how quickly you hear back: review begins only once the file is complete, so send everything together, clearly labelled, with English translations where applicable. Send each transcript as one complete file, not page by page — a transcript that arrives as separate images has to be reassembled before review can start, and that is the most common cause of delay. Send test scores to Drury directly from the exam provider's portal — screenshots and student copies are not accepted. When all required documents arrive and meet those standards, we can usually return an admission and scholarship decision within 10 working days.

ENGLISH TEST SCORES

Send your score to Drury University directly from the testing provider’s portal — IELTS, TOEFL, Duolingo or your test’s equivalent. A screenshot or student copy is not accepted as the official record.

BANK DOCUMENTS

A statement or letter on the bank’s official letterhead, dated within the last six months, showing the account holder’s name, the currency, and an available balance covering your first year. Credit card limits and transaction histories are not accepted.

PHASE ONE

Admission

Steps 1–5
  1. 1

    Apply

    Create a free account and start your application. There is no application fee, and you can save and return to it. Use the name exactly as it appears in your passport, because it must match your I-20 later.

    Free application →(opens in a new tab)
  2. 2

    Submit required documents

    Upload them in the application portal or email them to iadmissions@drury.edu. Any document not in English must be translated by a professional translation agency, and transcripts should arrive complete and clearly organized, with grades and subjects legible and every year of study included. Unofficial copies are accepted for review; official documents are required later.

    See requirements →
  3. 3

    Application review

    Review begins only once every required document has arrived. If something is missing, your counselor will email you rather than hold the file silently, so watch the address you applied with.

  4. 4

    Admission decision

    Admission and scholarship decisions are usually made within 10 working days of a complete, correctly prepared file. Your scholarship is awarded at the same time, on your transcript and English proficiency, with no separate application.

  5. 5

    Review costs & scholarships

    Work through the cost of attendance against your scholarship so you know your estimated yearly investment before you commit. This figure is also what your financial documentation must support.

    View costs of attendance →
PHASE TWO

Post-admission

Steps 6–9
  1. 6

    Pay the enrollment deposit

    A non-refundable $500 deposit secures your seat and is credited to your student account after enrollment. Nothing in the visa process can begin until this is paid.

    How to pay →
  2. 7

    Complete the housing application

    All first-year students live on campus. Submit your housing contract so we can match you with a room and roommate. International students are placed before arrival, so you never land without a room.

    Housing contract →(opens in a new tab) Housing & meal plan rates (PDF) →(opens in a new tab)
  3. 8

    Receive your I-20

    Once your deposit and housing application are in, your I-20 is typically issued within 48 hours. The day it arrives, check your name, date of birth and program dates against your passport and review the financial figures; talk anything unclear through with your counselor.

  4. 9

    Review finances & insurance again

    Take a second look at estimated costs, payment options, and required international student insurance before you travel, so the first bill holds no surprises.

PHASE THREE

Arrival planning

Steps 10–13
  1. 10

    Schedule your F-1 visa interview

    Pay the I-901 SEVIS fee, keep the payment confirmation, and complete Form DS-160 to schedule your F-1 visa interview at the nearest U.S. embassy or consulate. Book the earliest date available even if it is inconvenient. Our team shares tips to help you prepare, and can reissue your I-20 for a later term if the interview does not go your way.

    Check current visa wait times →(opens in a new tab)
  2. 11

    Academic advising & course selection

    Once your visa is approved, we send you a Pre-Scheduling Questionnaire (PSQ) so an academic advisor can build your first-semester schedule. Registration order follows the date each questionnaire arrives, so return yours as soon as you receive it.

  3. 12

    Complete required forms

    The Dean of Students Office will notify you of health, conduct and other required forms to complete before arrival.

    Required forms for new students →(opens in a new tab)
  4. 13

    Share your arrival information

    Submit your flight details so the International Support Services team can arrange airport pickup and welcome you to campus.

    Arrival information form →(opens in a new tab)
