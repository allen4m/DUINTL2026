Admission Requirements

First-Year Requirements

Four documents, and no application fee.

High school transcript
An unofficial transcript is accepted for admission. Documents not in English must be translated by a professional translation agency.
An English test score
IELTS UKVI or Academic, TOEFL iBT, Duolingo, SAKAE English Test, SAT, or ACT. Send scores to Drury through the official exam portal for the fastest processing.
Bank documents
Proof of funds for the first year, issued within the last six months. Transaction histories and credit lines are not accepted. We may ask for third-party verification.
Passport copy
The photo page including the machine-readable area, matching the name on your application. The passport must be valid for more than six months.
Attended college-level coursework? Submit a course-by-course evaluation report from a NACES-certified agency such as ECE, The Evaluation Company (TEC), or WES.

Springfield and Campus

The Big Small Town

Springfield is the birthplace of Route 66 and the heartland of American culture — Midwestern charm, a safe and temperate climate, and Ozark streams and lakes just minutes from campus. There is something on every weekend: the farmers market, Artsfest, fall festivals, Renaissance fairs, and festivals from around the world all year round. Springfield Cardinals baseball is a short walk away, and Springfield-Branson National Airport connects students home through major U.S. hubs.

Come Home to Campus

Across 90+ acres, you will dive into college life to the fullest — and before you know it, feel right at home. From Panther Palooza and Late-Night Breakfast to the Final Roar, the moments that bring our community together are what make Drury special. With 100+ clubs, organizations, and teams, there is a place for every person and every passion. Which three, four, five, or more will you join?
