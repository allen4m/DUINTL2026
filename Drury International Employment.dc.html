Before you work

Every type of employment below requires authorization before the first day of work. Some is authorized by your Designated School Official at Drury; some only by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services. Nothing is retroactive.

IMPORTANT

Working without authorization is a status violation. It can end your F-1 or J-1 record, affect future visa applications, and cannot be corrected after the fact. If you are unsure whether an opportunity counts as employment — including unpaid internships and paid work for an employer outside the United States — ask before you begin.

AUTHORIZED AT DRURY

On-campus employment, CPT, and J-1 Academic Training are handled by your DSO or Responsible Officer here.

AUTHORIZED BY USCIS

OPT and the STEM OPT extension require an application to USCIS and an Employment Authorization Document before you start.

Curricular Practical Training (CPT)

CPT is off-campus work that is an integral part of your program of study — an internship tied to a course or degree requirement. It is authorized by your DSO, not USCIS, and must be approved before you begin.

  • ›
    One full academic year of enrollment first
    Required before CPT, with limited exceptions for graduate programs that require immediate participation.
  • ›
    The work must be part of your curriculum
    Tied to a specific course or degree requirement, with faculty confirmation.
  • ›
    Employer-specific and date-specific
    Your I-20 is updated with the employer and the exact authorized dates. Changing employer requires new authorization.
  • ›
    Twelve months of full-time CPT affects OPT
    One full year or more of full-time CPT eliminates OPT eligibility at that degree level. Part-time CPT does not.

Optional Practical Training (OPT)

OPT is work authorization in your field of study, most often used after graduation. It requires an application to USCIS and an approved Employment Authorization Document before you may start.

12 months
STANDARD OPT

Available per degree level, used before or after completing your program. Work must relate to your major.

+24 months
STEM OPT EXTENSION

For eligible STEM degrees, with an E-Verify employer and a formal training plan. Applied for before your initial OPT expires.

Applications may be filed in a window around your program end date, and USCIS processing takes time — students who file late lose weeks of work eligibility. Your DSO must recommend OPT in SEVIS before you file.

J-1 Academic Training

Exchange students on a J-1 visa use Academic Training rather than CPT or OPT. It is authorized in writing by the Responsible Officer or Alternate Responsible Officer, must relate to your field of study, and is approved before the work begins.

Social Security number

You need a Social Security number to be paid, but you do not need one to accept a job offer. Apply once you have a job offer and the authorization that goes with it.
