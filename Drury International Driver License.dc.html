What is required

To apply for a Missouri driver license you must take a written test and a driving test. The Missouri Department of Revenue website(opens in a new tab) has more information and a copy of the Missouri State Driver’s Manual.

What to bring

  1. Passport with U.S. visa.
  2. I-94 card or online printout(opens in a new tab).
  3. Your most recent I-20 for F-1 students, or DS-2019 for J-1 students.
  4. A currently enrolled letter from the Registrar’s Office (an enrolment verification).
  5. Proof of residency.
  6. Social Security documentation: Bring your Social Security card or another acceptable document showing your full Social Security number (such as a W-2, SSA-1099, non-SSA-1099, or pay stub).
    If you have not been assigned a Social Security number, obtain a No Social Security Number verification letter (also called an SSA refusal/ineligibility letter, Form SSA-L676) from the Social Security Administration. Bring this letter along with your valid passport, visa, and most recent I-94 record.
    For F-2 or J-2 dependents, they must take the I-20 or DS-2019 with them and a letter of current enrollment stating their spouse is a full-time student of Drury.
