Discover the Drury Difference

Your resource on the Drury campus

Whether you are just starting to explore U.S. universities or are ready to apply, Allen is your main contact for all things Drury International.

He leads international admission, evaluates academic scholarships, handles I-20s as a Designated School Official and DS-2019s as an Alternate Responsible Officer, processes work authorizations, and works with partners and counselors around the world. If you have questions about programs, scholarships, visas, or life in Springfield, Missouri, this is the best place to start.

Who Allen works with

First-year international students Including those graduating from a U.S. high school.
International transfer students Transferring from an institution inside or outside the United States.
U.S. citizens and permanent residents living overseas Residing abroad, or graduating from a high school outside the United States.
DoDEA students Students attending schools in the Department of Defense Education Activity network.

What to ask about

Admission & scholarships How to apply, what documents to submit, and how academic scholarships work for international students.
Cost & financial planning Understanding tuition, fees, housing, and realistic yearly budgets.
I-20 & F-1 visa Timelines for getting your I-20, preparing for the visa interview, and what to expect when you arrive.
Programs & outcomes Choosing between Architecture, Business, Engineering, Computer Science, and other featured programs, and how Drury Fusion works in practice.
Student life Housing options, campus safety, clubs, and what it is like to live in Springfield.
